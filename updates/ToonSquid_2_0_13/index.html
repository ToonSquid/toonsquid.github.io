ToonSquid 2.0.13

27 September 2025


Bone Binding Improvements

In addition to editing the bindings between bones and layers directly on the canvas, you can now also edit the bindings using a layer list in the bones effect inspector. This is especially useful when you want to bind a bone to a group layer, which can be difficult to select on the canvas.
