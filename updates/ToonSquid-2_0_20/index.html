ToonSquid 2.0.20

13 February 2026


ToonSquid 2.0.20 comes with a floating color picker and the ability to duplicate and copy multiple selected drawings in multiple animation layers at once.

Floating Color Picker#

Drag the handle at the top of the editor's color picker to convert it into a compact floating color picker which you can place anywhere on your canvas.

Copy Drawings in Multiple Layers#

You can now copy and paste a selection of multiple drawings even if the drawings are in different animation layers.

Duplicate Multiple Drawings#

The duplicate button in the timeline will now duplicate all selected drawings at once.

Other Improvements#

  • If you accidentally tap to dismiss the share options after an export has finished without saving the file, ToonSquid will now ask you whether you would like to see the share options again, rather than immediately discarding the already exported file.
  • Flipping the canvas now flips it around the center of the visible region of the canvas on the screen so that whatever was previously in focus remains in focus.
  • The "X" close button in the top right corner of the export screen has been replaced with a "Cancel" button next to the "Export" button.
  • Fixed occasional crashes when pinching to zoom on the canvas while a vector layer and vector stroke brush is selected.
  • Fixed the unreliabilities of the mesh tools that were introduced with the previous update.
  • Various other bug fixes and reliability improvements.
