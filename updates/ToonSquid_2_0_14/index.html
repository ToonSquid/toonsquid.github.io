ToonSquid 2.0.14

29 October 2025


Chinese Localization

ToonSquid is now available in Simplified Chinese!

ToonSquid will automatically show the Chinese interface if that is the preferred language on your iPad. If your iPad is set to Chinese but you would like to continue to use ToonSquid in English, you can change the language in the iPad Settings app under Apps > ToonSquid > Language.

If you would like to suggest improvements for the Chinese localization, please reach out via contact@toonsquid.com. Your feedback is much appreciated!

Please note that the ToonSquid handbook and customer support via e-mail continue to be exclusively offered in English.
