ToonSquid 2.0.26

27 August 2026


ToonSquid 2.0.26 introduces the versatile Turbulent Displace effect and improves compatibility of exported PSDs with third party software.

Turbulent Displace#

Use the new Turbulent Displace effect to create a variety of visual effects such as line boiling, water ripples, fire and more.

Line Boil#

The default settings of Turbulent Displace create a line boiling effect. Just add the effect to your layer and you are all set.
